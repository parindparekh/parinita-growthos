TikTok short video and podcast scope

POST /v1/content/{id}/video creates an authenticated, downloadable preview from
release title, summary/body and CTA. The console offers Create 15-second video.
The initial renderer uses three five-second caption cards, 720x1280, 30 fps,
H.264 MP4. It is silent: no generated footage, narration or licensed music.
FFmpeg with drawtext and libx264 support is required. The Dockerfile installs
FFmpeg and fonts. Local operators may set FFMPEG_BINARY to an installed binary.
Rendering uses temporary files, a timeout and one concurrent render per process.

New TikTok destinations default to media_type=video. The governed delivery
path creates the clip and transfers it with the destination's credential to
the TikTok creator inbox using video.upload. The creator must open TikTok to
review and complete publication. A successful transfer is not marked published.
No live TikTok account transfer has been verified. Existing photo destinations
retain their behavior; explicitly set media_type=video to switch them.

Each company needs its own source connections and destination credentials.
Credential references alone do not implement tenant isolation. The current
application still needs company-scoped identity and account onboarding before
hosting unrelated companies in one instance.

GrowthOS handles audio podcast scripts, audio generation through an authorized
voice provider, and distribution via podcast RSS and connected hosting accounts.
Spotify and Apple show submission/ownership verification is separate.
Video podcasts are created in AI Studio. An automatic AI Studio handoff is not
implemented until its service contract and authorization are available.
